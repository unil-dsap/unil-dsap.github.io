Calling functions and writing your own: def, return and None, returning several values as a tuple. Signatures and arguments: positional, keyword, defaults. How workshops work

Lectures teach the language. In every workshop you run the same six steps, with an AI assistant of your choice:

  1. Framesay what you want, in plain words
  2. Askgive that brief to your AI assistant
  3. Runrun what it wrote
  4. Readfind the lines that do the real work
  5. Judgedecide whether to believe the output
  6. Keepcommit what works
