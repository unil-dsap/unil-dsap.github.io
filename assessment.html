Assessment

How the grade is built — and where the binding text lives.

The grade

Midterm
One third of the grade Session 7, November 2 — multiple choice, sat on Moodle, in class, in person, proctored. The exact conditions are confirmed before the semester begins.
Final exam
Two thirds of the grade January exam session, date set by the faculty — multiple choice, sat on Moodle, in person, proctored. The exact conditions are confirmed before the semester begins.
Homework
Optional bonus, up to +0.5 One GitHub project, autograded, built in stages. The bonus applies only if the two exams alone average 4.0 or better — it can raise a good grade, never rescue a failed one. Details and the exact bonus scale arrive with the assignment around the midterm.

How the papers are scored

Guessing
Multiple choice, corrected for guessing Points start above the number of correct answers expected from answering at random. The exact scale is stated on the exam itself. Answering everything is always the right strategy — random guessing simply earns zero, not a penalty.
What is tested
Reading, tracing, and judging code What does this print; which line is the bug; is this output plausible; what was missing from this brief. Every question type is practiced in the weekly workshops.
AI
Not available during the exam Both exams are sat in person, in a supervised room. Everywhere else in the course, AI assistants are allowed and expected.

Also on Moodle

Mirror
Everything on this page is posted on the course Moodle as well Exam announcements, preparation material, and deadlines are published on Moodle and here, at the same time. If the two ever differ, Moodle is the official channel.

The binding document

Study plan
The legally binding assessment terms In the event of an appeal, only the study plan (plan d'études) is binding — not this page, not the syllabus. The official modalities text will be linked here once approved by the faculty. Until then, this page is an accurate summary of the intended terms.
